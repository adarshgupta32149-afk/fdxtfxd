Your data & backups

Permanent storage (recommended): click 🔗 Connect a permanent data file in the bar at the top and pick a spot like your Documents folder. From then on every change saves straight into that real file on your disk — clearing your browser cannot erase it. When you reopen the app, click Reconnect my data file once and you're back.

Extra safety copy: now and then click ⬇ Backup (top right) to save a dated .json copy on a USB drive or another folder. Use ⬆ Restore to load it back or move to another computer.

Keep the stock-tracker.html file anywhere and double-click to open it any time. For the permanent-file feature, open it in Chrome or Edge.

Note: if you haven't connected a data file, your data sits in browser storage, which clearing browser data or incognito mode can wipe — so connect a file or keep backups.
